Shuls with Focused doors have two small devices at the entrance. Your phone notices which one is closer, so it knows the moment you step inside.

Focused doors

At a shul with Focused doors, your phone switches as you pass the door, usually within seconds. Walk out and the ringer comes back.

Shuls near you

Focused finds the shuls around you on its own, with no setup. Arrive and stay a moment, and it switches to vibrate. Turn off any shul you don't want, like the one next door.

Your own places and times

Add your office, a library or a weekly shiur. Pick a place, a time like weekdays 9 to 5, or both. Choose vibrate or full silent for each one.

You stay in control

Your ringer always comes back.

Silencing a phone is the easy part. Focused works just as hard at giving your ringer back, so you never miss calls for the rest of the day.

  • One tap to restoreA notification shows whenever Focused is holding your ringer. Tap Restore ringer and it's back at once.
  • Pause everythingOne switch turns all of Focused off until you turn it back on.
  • Your choice winsAlready put your phone on silent? Focused leaves it alone. It never makes your phone louder than you set it.

Questions
Does it work at a shul without Focused doors?

Yes. Focused finds nearby shuls by location and switches when you arrive and stay a moment. Focused doors make it faster and exact to the doorway.

Will I miss an emergency call?

No. At shul, Focused only switches your phone to vibrate. Calls and messages still come through and your phone still buzzes, it just doesn't ring out loud. Full silent is only used at places of your own that you choose to set to silent.

Why does it ask for location "all the time"?

Nobody opens an app while walking through a door. Focused has to notice you arrive while your phone is in your pocket. Android also counts Bluetooth scanning as location.

Will it drain my battery?

No. Focused doesn't keep tracking your location. It stays asleep, and Android wakes it only when you arrive at a shul or place you've set, and again when you leave. At a shul with Focused doors, your phone just listens for the door's Bluetooth signal in short, low-power bursts.

My phone didn't switch. What should I check?

Open Focused and finish every step on the Setup page. On Samsung and Xiaomi phones, also turn off battery saving for Focused.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Focused is Android only for now. An iPhone version is in development.
