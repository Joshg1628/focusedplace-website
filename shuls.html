Setup

Ready in an afternoon.

    Each pair is programmed with your shul's own number before we install them, so phones know which shul they're at.

  2. 2

    One inside and one outside, mirrored on either side of the door. A side entrance gets its own outside device.

  3. 3

    Your shul goes live

    Your shul shows "Focused door" in the app. From then on, phones switch right at your door.

Your congregants' privacy

The devices collect nothing.

Focused doors only send out a short signal a few times a second. They don't connect to anyone's phone, they don't receive anything, and they don't record who walks past.

Your shul learns nothing about who came or when. Each person's phone decides for itself, and their app needs no account.
